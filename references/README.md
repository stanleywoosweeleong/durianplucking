# References

Sources behind the figures in the app. The Malaysia DOA guideline is the app's default;
a figure of the app's own is kept only where one of the sources below supports it.

Each paper here was downloaded from its publisher and read before the app cited it.
They are redistributed unchanged under the licence shown. If you reuse them, keep the
attribution and respect the licence — the two CC BY-NC-ND papers may not be used
commercially or modified.

## Looked for and not found

- **UC Davis Postharvest Center durian page** — the site refused the download, so its
  13–15°C recommendation is no longer cited in the app.
- **A source for Chanee maturing at about 90 days** — the app used to attribute this to
  *Scientific Reports*. That paper is about Monthong peduncles and does not say it. The
  figure has been removed; Chanee now follows the ITFNet table (100–115 days).
- **Clone-specific maturity days for Tekka, IOI, Red Prawn, Red Flesh** — no published figure
  found. For Black Thorn there are only the Betong grower's 115 days and the Thai study's
  105-day cut; its 115–128 day window remains the app's estimate. These clones show the DOA general guide (about day 90), which
  is a reference point and not a minimum.

## Figures in the app with no source

These are stated in the app but rest on no source listed above. The app labels the first
two groups as estimates; the rest carry a short caveat.

- The over-ripe line for every clone (drop window + about 8 days).
- Drop windows for Black Thorn, Tekka, IOI, Red Prawn, Red Flesh, kampung and "other".
- The 85/90/95% dial's conversion to days before drop (12/8/4), the boundaries between the
  four flavour bands, the allowance either side of a projected date, and the cold-chain days
  for dropped fruit. The app no longer shows flavour as a percentage, because no study
  validates one.
- Lab-panel threshold values (dry matter percentages, firmness ranges).
- Transit days and ASEAN Express details, which come from news and trade reports.
- Everything attributed to the DOA guideline, which is unpublished and not in this repo.
