Some argue Malaysia should adopt Thai-style cut-harvest to survive; others hold that tree-ripening is the flavour signature that sets Malaysia apart. This tool takes no side. It uses science to show, for each clone, how close to natural drop you can safely cut, how much flavour that costs, and how much shelf life it buys — the decision stays yours, and depends on your market and this season's conditions.

为什么这个决定开始变得关键Why this decision is starting to matter

树熟果常温下约 1–2 天便会开裂（取肉后冷藏保鲜，或冷冻可存数月）。出口中国有几条路：空运约1天、能卖「树熟」溢价但运费高；冷藏海运约5–9天，成本低走量大；新开的「东盟快铁」（雪兰莪→重庆）约9天、以急冻为主。除空运外都需要提前一点采割来撑过在途 —— 这正是割果时机开始重要的地方。当本地市场被同季爆量淹没，果无处可去只能贱卖。就算没有区域性影响，随着大批规模化果园陆续投产，产量本身也会在国内造成「榴梿海啸」 —— 这不再是偶发，而是结构性的。无论今季价格是否回稳，能把成熟度判断建立在科学、而非猜测上的果农，才更有底气面对下一次爆量。这正是这个工具存在的原因。 Tree-drop fruit cracks open in only 1–2 days at room temp (once opened, refrigerate the flesh, or freeze it to keep for months). Export to China runs a few ways: by air (~1 day, sells the tree-ripe premium but costs more); reefer sea (~5–9 days, cheaper and higher-volume); and the new ASEAN Express rail (Selangor→Chongqing, ~9 days, mostly frozen). Every mode but air needs cutting a little earlier to survive transit — which is exactly where harvest timing starts to matter. When the domestic market is flooded by a simultaneous glut, fruit has nowhere to go but a fire-sale. Even without any regional oversupply, as large-scale plantations keep coming into production, sheer volume will trigger a "durian tsunami" domestically — this is no longer a one-off, it's structural. Whether or not prices steady this season, the growers who ground their maturity decisions in science rather than guesswork will be the ones ready for the next glut. That is exactly why this tool exists.

1榴梿是呼吸跃变型水果Durian is a climacteric fruit01:16

榴梿属呼吸跃变型（climacteric）果实：一旦在树上达到生理成熟，即使离树也能继续后熟。后熟期间，果实呼吸骤升（大量释放二氧化碳），乙烯生成激增。 Durian is a climacteric fruit: once it reaches physiological maturity on the tree, it keeps ripening even after detachment. During this, respiration spikes (releasing CO₂) and ethylene production surges.

2乙烯：成熟的开关Ethylene: the ripening switch01:51

台湾植物生理学家杨祥发博士在1980年代破解了植物合成乙烯的「杨氏循环（Yang Cycle）」，奠定了果实催熟与保鲜的基础。乙烯这一关键激素，驱动果肉软化、淀粉转化为糖、以及标志性香气的形成。 Taiwanese plant physiologist Dr. Shang Fa Yang decoded how plants synthesize ethylene — the Yang Cycle (1980s) — laying the foundation for fruit ripening and preservation. This key hormone drives flesh softening, starch-to-sugar conversion, and signature aroma formation.

「催熟能让果实开裂，但开裂只是外表信号 —— 不代表里面的果肉真的成熟。」"Artificial ethylene can force cracking, but cracking is only an external sign — it doesn't mean the flesh inside is truly ripe."

3生理成熟 ≠ 好吃Physiological maturity ≠ quality03:31

树上达到「生理成熟」，只是果实能正常后熟的最低门槛。若采得太早，采后后熟会微弱或不完整。1990年代泰国研究已在金枕头（Monthong）与青尼（Chanee）等品种上证实这些跃变特性。 Reaching physiological maturity on the tree is only the minimum threshold for proper off-tree ripening. Plucked too early, post-harvest ripening is weak or incomplete. 1990s Thai research confirmed these climacteric traits in Monthong and Chanee.

自然熟 vs 催熟：自然成熟的果实始终有更软的果肉、更好的淀粉转糖、更高糖度。越贴近该品种的落果窗口采收，自然与催熟的品质差距就越小。 Natural vs artificial: naturally ripened fruit consistently gives softer flesh, better starch-to-sugar conversion, and higher sugar. The closer to that variety's drop window you harvest, the smaller the quality gap becomes.

马来西亚 ≠ 泰国做法：大马榴梿多为树熟落网（约130 DAA 落果），常温约1–2天即开裂；泰国则在生理成熟时剪采（青尼约90、金枕约105 DAA）以利运输出口。每个品种的成熟天数不同 —— 见「采收决策」中各品种的落果窗口。 Malaysia ≠ Thailand: Malaysian durians are mostly tree-dropped into nets (~130 DAA), cracking open in ~1–2 days at room temp; Thailand cuts at physiological maturity (Chanee ~90, Monthong ~105 DAA) to enable export. Each clone's maturity differs — see the per-clone drop windows in the Harvest Decision tab.

✓关键：存在「安全割果窗口」Key: a "safe cut window" exists

这是本工具的科学核心。MARDI 对猫山王（D197）的研究显示：在 15 周（约105 DAA）割下的果，其干物质、糖度（约40 °Brix）、果肉颜色、软硬度与感官口味，与自然落果果几乎一致；而更早的 13–14 周则明显不足。 This is the tool's scientific core. MARDI's study on Musang King (D197) found that fruit cut at 15 weeks (~105 DAA) matched naturally-abscised fruit in dry matter, sugar (~40 °Brix), pulp colour, firmness and sensory taste — while earlier 13–14 week fruit fell clearly short.

换句话说：并非「割果 = 差」，而是「割得太早 = 差」。贴近落果窗口的迟割，能保住绝大部分风味，同时换来运输所需的货架期。这正是树熟与泰式早割之间的第三条路。 In other words: it's not "cutting = worse," it's "cutting too early = worse." A late cut close to the drop window keeps most of the flavour while buying the shelf life transport needs. This is the third path between tree-drop and Thai-style early cutting.

许多果农已在做的「14–17周吊网」正是这个原理：让果留树到接近成熟，落下时由网接住，而非直接落地。本工具把这个判断量化到每个品种。The "week 14–17 tie-netting" many growers already practise works on exactly this principle: keep fruit on the tree near maturity, catch it in a net instead of letting it hit the ground. This tool quantifies that judgment per clone.

5核心差异与未来指标Core difference & future markers07:56

最新研究发现，随着成熟，果柄中的天冬酰胺显著上升 —— 目前虽只是实验室指标，未来或成为检测精确成熟度的生化标记。 Recent studies show aspartic acid / asparagine in the stalk rises sharply with maturity — currently a lab metric, but potentially a future biochemical maturity marker.

树熟果在离树前，已从母树累积了最大干物质、淀粉与香气前体；采摘果则只能依赖被切下那一刻已储备的资源。Tree-ripe fruit accumulates maximum dry matter, starch, and aroma precursors from the mother tree before peak ripeness; plucked fruit relies only on what it had stored the moment it was cut.

%「风味保留率」是怎么算的How "flavour retention %" is calculated

先说清楚：这是一个模型估算，不是实验室实测。它的形状与锚点有研究依据，但每个百分比是锚点之间的线性插值，方便你直观比较不同割果时机 —— 不代表「割在90%就一定保留84%风味」。真正的校准，靠你自己的感官记录。 Up front: this is a model estimate, not a lab measurement. Its shape and anchor points are research-based, but each percentage is a linear interpolation between those anchors — a way to compare cut timings at a glance, not a claim that "cutting at 90% retains exactly 84% of flavour." Real calibration comes from your own sensory log.

锚点（相对自然落果 = 100%）Anchor points (vs natural drop = 100%)
· 自然落果后（drop+）：100% 风味（峰值基准）
· 落果窗口内（dropHi）：≈ 99%

锚点的研究依据：What the anchors rest on:
① 自然落果 = 风味峰值 —— 大马业界与多份文献一致（落果果「creamy、pungent、peak ripeness」）。
② 近落果剪采 ≈ 落果品质 —— MARDI D197：约15周（≈落果）剪采，在干物质、糖度、颜色、硬度、感官上与落果果相当；更早则不足。
③ 早割风味显著变差 —— 综述文献：未成熟果「不能正常后熟，风味明显变差」。
④ 成熟度可量化 —— 金枕头成熟指数研究（ISHS/Somsri）用感官评级 50%→90%，与干物质高度相关（R²=0.8）。
① Natural drop = peak flavour — Malaysian practice + multiple sources (dropped fruit is "creamy, pungent, peak ripeness").
② Near-drop cut ≈ abscised quality — MARDI D197: ~15 WAA (≈drop) cut matches dropped fruit in dry matter, TSS, colour, firmness, sensory; earlier falls short.
③ Early cutting clearly worse — reviews: immature fruit "won't ripen properly, substantially less flavour."
④ Maturity is quantifiable — Monthong maturity-index study (ISHS/Somsri) ranked 50%→90% by sensory, strongly correlated with dry matter (R²=0.8).
